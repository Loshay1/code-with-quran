Companion texts shown with the Qur'an in the browser reader.
Both are copied verbatim by scripts/build-companions.js. Nothing is added,
removed or changed; the reader only formats them on screen (it hides the
"(n) " verse label in the translation and renders "[n]" as a footnote link).

The Qur'an itself is the Tanzil Uthmani text: see QURAN-TEXT-LICENSE.txt.

transliteration-en.json  (offline fallback)

When online, the reader shows the Quran.com word-by-word transliteration
instead, fetched live per surah by src/qurancom.js and held in memory only.
It is never bundled or written to disk: Quran Foundation's developer terms
(https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms, updated
2026-10-11) forbid redistributing its content as a dataset or build-time
bundle and storing it for more than a week.

Transliteration: Tanzil Project, English transliteration
  (en.transliteration), https://tanzil.net/trans/en.transliteration
Snapshot used: npm quran-json@3.1.2, dist/quran_transliteration.json
  (dataset by Risan Bagja Pradana, CC BY-SA 4.0)

Terms: Tanzil publishes its translations and transliterations for
non-commercial use. Commercial use needs permission from the publisher.
Check https://tanzil.net/trans/ for current terms.
